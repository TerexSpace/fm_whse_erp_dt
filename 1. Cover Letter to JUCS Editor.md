"Federated Modular Cloud ERP with Blockchain Consensus and Digital Twin Integration: A Novel Framework for SME Warehouse Management Optimization"

MANUSCRIPT OVERVIEW:

This work addresses critical challenges faced by Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in adopting comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems—specifically high costs, computational constraints, and data privacy concerns. We present FM-ERP (Federated Modular Cloud ERP), a novel architectural framework that integrates four cutting-edge technologies within a unified system:

1. Blockchain consensus mechanisms (Proof-of-Data-Quality)
2. Federated learning for privacy-preserving distributed AI
3. Digital twin technology for real-time warehouse simulation
4. IoT sensor networks for automated data capture

SIGNIFICANCE & NOVELTY:

Our framework represents the first comprehensive integration of blockchain, federated learning, and digital twins specifically designed for SME constraints. Unlike monolithic ERP systems, FM-ERP enables collaborative optimization across multiple SME warehouses without requiring raw operational data sharing, thus maintaining data sovereignty while achieving collective intelligence.

Key technical contributions include:

• **Novel Consensus Mechanism**: Proof-of-Data-Quality (PoDQ) - a reputation-based consensus achieving 2.3-second finality with 15 participating SME nodes, dramatically faster than Proof-of-Work (600s) or Proof-of-Stake (85s)

• **Hybrid Optimization Algorithm**: AQPSO-BV (Adaptive Quantum-Inspired Particle Swarm Optimization with Blockchain Verification) - combining metaheuristic search with cryptographic validation for federated resource allocation

• **Federated Digital Twins**: Distributed warehouse simulation protocol enabling multi-party optimization without centralized coordination

• **Seven-Layer Modular Architecture**: Ports-and-adapters pattern ensuring extensibility and technology-agnostic integration

EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION:

We validate FM-ERP using synthetic warehouse datasets simulating 15 SME nodes with heterogeneous operations (retail, manufacturing, logistics). Results demonstrate:

• 42% reduction in inventory discrepancies
• 38% improvement in order fulfillment efficiency  
• 51% reduction in computational overhead vs. centralized approaches
• 99.7% data privacy preservation through federated learning
• Linear scalability from 10 to 50 participating nodes
